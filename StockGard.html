StockGard scores every item against your lead time and safety stock, separates true stockouts from low stock, and hands your buyer a queue sorted into five buckets — Act Now first.

Projected run-out dates from open sales and purchase lines — see what runs out before it does
Oversold, backorders, and true stockouts (nothing on hand) separated and prioritized
Five buckets: Act Now / Reorder / Healthy / Excess & Obsolete / Housekeeping
Scored on inventory position (On Hand + Open PO − Open SO) — committed demand counts
Export to Excel — one tab per bucket + All Items + Run Notes
Runs entirely in your browser — no data leaves your machine
